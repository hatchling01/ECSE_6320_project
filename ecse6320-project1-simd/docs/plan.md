# Project Plan

Phases 0–6 are complete. Phase 6 is the final report, requirement audit, and repository reproducibility check; it collected no new benchmark or profile data.

- **Phase 0 — COMPLETE:** repository, specification, environment, hypotheses.
- **Phase 1 — COMPLETE:** kernels, reference implementations, correctness.
- **Phase 2 — COMPLETE:** isolated compiler variants, correctness reruns, vectorization reports, targeted disassembly, and SIMD verification matrix.
- **Phase 3 — COMPLETE:** calibrated pilot, bounded working-set sweep, scalar/auto/native GCC matrix, statistics, speedups, datatype comparison, effective bandwidth, and essential plots/tables.
- **Phase 3B — COMPLETE:** variability diagnosis, bounded selective reruns, merged-source statistics, and N-dependent dot-product error-bound validation.
- **Phase 4A — COMPLETE:** alignment-only measurements for AXPY and elementwise multiply, both types, GCC scalar/auto/native, three Phase 3 working sets, and three pointer-residue conditions. Five initial samples per configuration; one additional five-sample rerun for each initial CV >20% configuration. Raw files are separate and CV remains high in some cases.
- **Phase 4B — COMPLETE:** AXPY float32 tail-only matrix for GCC scalar/auto/native; two cache-resident bases × remainders 0–15 × three builds = 96 configurations. Five initial samples each; one additional five-sample rerun for 14 configurations above 20% initial CV. No systematic native penalty was resolved because variability exceeded the expected added-work cost.
- **Phase 4C — COMPLETE:** fixed-span float32 strided elementwise multiply for GCC scalar/auto/native; three spans × strides 1/2/4/8 × three builds = 36 configurations. Five initial samples each; one selective five-sample rerun for the 13 configurations above 20% initial CV. Non-unit strides use scalar cleanup paths in GCC auto/native disassembly, so SIMD speedups collapse; useful bandwidth also falls as stride rises.
- **Phase 4D — COMPLETE:** consolidated two evidence-based negative cases and seven benchmark pitfalls from existing Phase 3B/4C measurements, Phase 2 disassembly, and Phase 3B dot-accuracy validation. No new benchmark sweep was run.
- **Phase 4 — COMPLETE:** alignment, tail, stride, and negative-case evidence documented.
- **Phase 5A — COMPLETE:** nine representative AMD uProf profiles for float32 elementwise multiply (GCC scalar/auto/native × three Phase 3B working sets), with cycles, retired instructions, IPC/CPI, width-specific FP-uop, L1, L2-request, and L1 DTLB events. No direct L3/DF/UMC or memory-controller bandwidth counters were available; no roofline analysis was performed.
- **Phase 5B — COMPLETE:** useful-byte arithmetic intensities, a large-working-set Phase 3B effective streaming-bandwidth reference, an explicitly assumed theoretical single-core ceiling, and a native GCC roofline-style synthesis. No new benchmark, copy study, or uProf profile was required; no physical DRAM roofline is claimed.
- **Phase 5 — COMPLETE:** representative profiling and useful-byte roofline synthesis documented.
- **Phase 6 — COMPLETE:** consolidated the final README, mapped the original hypotheses to outcomes, audited assignment requirements and limitations, indexed 12 existing figures and 13 existing tables, documented data provenance/reproduction/AI use, and ran lightweight final validation. No benchmark, uProf profile, or new plot was generated.

Phase 1 passed 448 correctness cases. Phase 2 created the isolated compiler variants and code-generation evidence. Phase 3 ran the elementwise-only pilot and a corrected 3,600-sample GCC matrix across 15 target sizes. `phase3_raw_v3.csv` is canonical; v1 is retained as superseded and v2 as partial. Phase 3B reran the top 40 CV>20 configurations once, preserved original and rerun data separately, and generated merged statistics. Variability remains high in some configurations; 190/360 merged configurations remain above 10% CV. Eighteen dot accuracy checks passed the documented N-dependent bound, though the largest float32 bound is non-informative. Phase 4A collected 540 initial alignment samples plus 175 targeted rerun samples; its 31/108 configurations above 20% CV remain flagged. Phase 4B collected 480 initial tail samples and 70 targeted rerun samples; 13/96 configurations remain above 20% CV and no systematic tail penalty was resolved. Phase 4C collected 180 initial stride samples plus 65 selective rerun samples, with 11/36 configurations still above 20% CV. Phase 4D consolidated existing evidence without a new experiment. Phase 5A collected nine representative uProf profiles and cross-checked them against Phase 3B; Phase 5B generated the useful-byte roofline synthesis without new measurements. Phase 6 completed the README report, audit, figure/table indexes, and lightweight verification without collecting new measurements.
